How the split works

Live uses dates on or after {{ cutover.cutover_date|date:"j M Y" }}; the Backlog uses earlier dates. Switching mode filters every date-tracked module, and a date on the wrong side is refused rather than filed silently in the wrong place.

Customer and supplier balances stay matched invoice by invoice across both periods, so unpaid backlog invoices remain collectable and payable in Live.

Merge makes Live continue from the backlog's closing position as though there had been no break, and can be undone. Archiving keeps the backlog sealed and separate.
