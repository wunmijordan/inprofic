How to read this
Revenue and cost come from paid sales; cost is the unit cost recorded when each sale was made, so past months are not repriced. "Cost per unit (current)" splits today's recipe into ingredients and production materials (packaging and other supplies) for products you make, or shows the latest purchase cost for resale items. Best sellers and top earners are the top few by units and by profit; low margin is below 20%; slow movers are in the bottom quarter of sellers.
