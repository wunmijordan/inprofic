How this is calculated
Revenue is paid sales. Cost of goods uses each sold item's recorded unit cost. Expenses count when recorded, paid or not. Payroll is the employer cost of issued payslips (gross pay plus employer contributions) on the pay date. Operating supplies used and stock write-offs (wastage, damage, count corrections, staff and business use) are included. Buying stock is not an expense until it is sold, used or written off. If you also record wages as a "Labour / Wages" expense, they appear in both lines — record them in one place.
