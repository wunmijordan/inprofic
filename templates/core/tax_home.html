VAT

Prices in Inprofic are treated as VAT-inclusive: VAT is extracted from the price a customer pays. Set each product's treatment (standard, zero-rated or exempt) under Product VAT treatment.

E-invoicing

Invoices are cleared with the tax authority through an accredited access-point provider. Inprofic prepares structured invoice data and tracks the IRN, stamp and status; map the data to your provider's schema before going live.
