Questions, answered

How INPROFIC fits the way you work.

From purchasing and production to storefronts, staff access and reporting, these are the questions businesses most often need answered before bringing their operation into one place.

What kinds of businesses can use INPROFIC?+

INPROFIC supports bakeries, restaurants and food service, general production businesses, wholesalers and retailers. The wording, selling channels and available workflows adjust to the service you choose.

Can I track raw materials in bags, cartons, kilograms, grams, litres or other units?+

Yes. Purchase units and usage units can be different. INPROFIC converts what you buy into the finer unit your recipes or formulas actually consume.

What if I buy only part of a bag, carton or other purchase unit?+

Fractional purchases are supported. Enter the quantity bought and the total amount paid; INPROFIC works out the equivalent whole-unit cost and updates stock valuation correctly.

Can products have recipes or formulas?+

Yes. Made-in-house products can define the materials used per production batch, including ingredients, packaging and other production inputs. Those relationships drive material planning and costing.

Can production be planned from a key ingredient such as rice or flour?+

Yes. A made-in-house product can optionally nominate one recipe item as its Base material. For stock production, you can enter how much of that material you want to use and INPROFIC calculates the matching production size and the quantities of the other recipe materials.

Can I still plan production by batches or product quantity?+

Yes. Base-material sizing is optional. The existing product-quantity and batch workflow remains available and works exactly as before.

Does INPROFIC track expected output, wastage and actual yield?+

Yes. Production completion records gross output, rejected or wasted units and saleable output, and shows the difference and yield against the production target.

Can I produce stock without assigning it to a customer first?+

Yes. Market-stock and store-replenishment production can be created without a customer. Completed output becomes available for later sale through the appropriate channel.

What happens when a customer pays for something that must still be made?+

Once payment is confirmed, the order moves into Intake and a pending production order is prepared. Staff then continue the normal production process; payment does not automatically start or complete production.

What happens when a customer pays for an item already in stock?+

Available stock is reserved while payment is pending. Once payment is confirmed, the reservation is committed and stock is deducted automatically, without a second manual acceptance step.

Can I sell products my business did not make?+

Yes. Production businesses can mark a sellable item as purchased for resale—for example bottled drinks in a restaurant. Those products are received through Procurement and can be sold alongside made-in-house products without entering production.

Can one sale contain both stocked and made-to-order items?+

Yes. A mixed basket can deduct stocked items after payment while sending made-to-order items into Intake for staff-controlled production.

Can I record several purchases or expenses at once?+

Yes. Procurement supports multi-item purchase orders, and Expenses supports compact bulk entry so several records can be saved together without repeating the whole process.

Does Procurement update both quantity and cost?+

Yes. Receiving a purchase updates the relevant stock balance and the latest cost used for valuation and production costing, with purchase history retained for reference.

Can different selling channels have different prices?+

Yes. Products can have channel-specific prices, and selected customers can also have agreed product prices where needed. The channel wording follows the type of business.

Is there an in-premise point of sale?+

Yes. Authorised staff can use a full-page selling screen on a touchscreen, laptop or desktop. It supports product search, cart quantities, customer/order context and cash or supported electronic payments.

Do POS staff need full Commerce access?+

No. In-Premise POS has its own role permission with View and Edit choices. A cashier-only user goes straight to the counter storefront and can log out from that screen.

Can delivery be handled inside INPROFIC?+

Yes. Delivery is plan-gated. Businesses can run their own rider fleet, use external delivery providers{% if glovo_platform_enabled %} such as Glovo LaaS v2{% endif %}, or use true Hybrid routing where supported methods remain available per order. Rider assignments, customer-visible switching policy, tracking, POD, exception alerts and provider webhook updates stay inside one delivery timeline.

Can external auditors query records?+

Yes. Auditors can raise questions from the Audit Workspace, while business admins and other permitted audit reviewers can respond and close those items without giving the auditor normal operational edit access.

Can customers order from an external storefront?+

Yes. Businesses can publish a storefront with product images, availability, channel prices and checkout, or connect their own website to the commerce flow.

How are online payments handled?+

Supported payment methods can include Paystack, Monnify and confirmed bank transfer. INPROFIC waits for trusted payment confirmation before committing a paid order.

What about cash payments at the counter?+

An authorised POS officer can mark Cash received only after the money is physically in hand. That confirmation then follows the same stock or production handoff as other confirmed payments.

Does INPROFIC handle credit sales and customer balances?+

Yes. Customer records can carry credit limits and payment terms, and unpaid sales remain visible until payment is recorded through Finance.

Can I track cash, bank and card balances?+

Yes. Finance accounts connect money received and paid to sales, purchases, expenses and other recorded movements so balances can be reconciled.

What reports are available?+

Reporting can bring together inventory, purchasing, production, sales, expenses and financial performance. The depth of reporting available depends on the chosen plan.

Can staff have different levels of access?+

Yes. Roles can control View and Edit access by area, with selected individual adjustments where appropriate. Business admins can manage ordinary team access while protected roles remain restricted.

Can INPROFIC alert staff about commerce activity?+

Yes. Commerce activity can appear in the in-app notification tray, with supported device notifications available when enabled.

Can I install INPROFIC like an app?+

Yes. Supported browsers can install the workspace to the device home screen for quicker access, while the same account remains available through the web.

Can I start small and add more later?+

Yes. Starter remains the focused one-user entry plan{% if starter_plan and starter_plan.is_free_forever %} and is currently free forever{% endif %}, while larger plans add founder-configured capacity and modules. Eligible promotions can apply to monthly or yearly payments.
