# INPROFIC product context

INPROFIC connects inventory, purchasing, production, sales, cash/finance,
commerce and delivery for bakeries, restaurants, general producers, wholesalers
and retailers. The application is a business-scoped Django monolith with server
rendered templates. Subscription entitlements and staff permissions govern
surfaces; interconnected stock, finance and audit records continue to synchronize.

Staff use the workspace repeatedly throughout a working day: entering purchases,
recording production and sales, reviewing shortages and settling balances. Dense
operational tables, explicit states and legible controls matter more than novelty.
Both light/dark themes and configured tenant palettes are supported.

Distinct workflows include proportional recipe release against registered yield,
Shared Production Runs that retain individual customer orders, separate demand /
planned offcut / unexpected excess / wastage / shortage, guided floor entry,
compensating reversal, payment-verified checkout, and controlled ledger cut-over.
Books are derived through explicit rebuilds. VAT and e-invoice preparation do not
guarantee provider clearance. Manual Daily brief text sharing remains separate
from approved-template automatic WhatsApp delivery.
