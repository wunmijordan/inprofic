# INPROFIC Q4 2026 Social Media Ads & Content Calendar

Generated from `docs/feature_marketing_catalog.json` by `scripts/generate_marketing_calendar.py`. The command keeps the detailed CSV, this Markdown summary and `docs/INPROFIC_Q4_2026_Content_Calendar.xlsx` synchronized.

## Campaign structure

- **1–7 October:** launch week — brand reveal, audience fit, connected workflow story and product tour.
- **8–31 October:** feature education — what each capability does.
- **November:** problem → workflow — revisit the same capabilities through real operating problems and connected workflows.
- **December:** control / ROI / readiness — revisit them around controls, evidence and next-period readiness without fabricated savings claims.
- Every normal week uses a seven-day lens: problem, how-it-works, screen/workflow demo, connected handoff, control/verification, persona/use case, and recap/CTA.
- Paid/boosted placements are mixed into the daily plan; every row includes feature group, weekly lens, format, channels, audience, hook, CTA and creative brief.

## Newly added capabilities highlighted in this release

- ✨ **Persistent stock alerts inside the shared operations tray** — added 2026-09-20: Receive specific raw-material and product warning/low counts through Web Push after stock changes commit, with per-user snooze/re-alert timing, registration/delivery checks and retries. Newly enabled devices receive current summaries; the shared in-app tray retains eight audio chimes.
- ✨ **Multi-vendor purchase requests, approval and sharing** — added 2026-11-04: Group items by optional saved or typed vendor names in one request, with separate payment status, method, account and advances for each group. Approve once, receive and settle each vendor independently, and share grouped items and payment breakdowns in the combined preview, PDF, paper snapshot and detailed text. Paid drafts require confirmation and preserve audited payment history through edits or deletion.
- ✨ **Weekday production colors and shelf life** — added 2026-10-20: Set recurring Monday–Sunday colors that automatically tag new orders by production date, with weekday tooltips. Optional shelf-life days suggest batch expiry; saved order tags and actual expiry dates stay historical. Backlog batches are excluded from current expiry warnings.
- ✨ **Output-weighted base-material production** — added 2026-10-19: Record finished unit weight on products, apportion a shared base-material pool across outputs by weight, release those shares at approval, and warn when planned output exceeds recipe capacity.
- ✨ **Receivables, payables and partial settlement** — added 2026-11-04: Record partial customer collections across Cash, Card and Transfer with a receiving account and reference per part. Finance retains the outstanding invoice balance until later receipts settle it; vendor purchase orders keep independent advances and balances.
- ✨ **Staff payroll add-on with shareable payslips** — added 2026-09-29: Link existing INPROFIC users or add non-user staff, configure pay structures plus tax, pension and other calculation rules, then issue frozen-detail payslips that can be shared by email or WhatsApp.
- ✨ **Hosted storefront, product choices, variants and add-ons** — added 2026-10-06: Publish tenant products with channel-safe pricing and group separately stocked, priced and produced size variants under one storefront card. Each product can offer a no-price-change choice such as flavor or color; separately priced add-ons remain their own basket lines. Customers see item readiness, add order notes, request pickup or delivery times, and get printable checkout and payment receipts using the business profile's logo, tagline and contact details after verified payment.
- ✨ **Manual transfer routes (no gateway)** — added 2026-09-20: Offer one or more business transfer accounts, let hosted/headless customers choose the destination, require payment proof externally, and keep payment pending until staff verifies the actual credit.
- ✨ **In-premise POS** — added 2026-09-24: Staff can complete counter sales with Cash, direct Transfer or supported terminal payment only when the tenant plan includes Commerce and Commerce is explicitly enabled; POS still keeps its dedicated user permission.
- ✨ **Provider-neutral delivery partner adapters** — added 2026-09-20: Keep INPROFIC in control of delivery pricing, routing and tracking while any courier stays manual or connects through a normalized dispatch/status adapter. When the platform enables the optional Shipbubble adapter, each business connects its own account and configures courier choice, parcel defaults and dispatch. Obtain software-level written permission before public use of Shipbubble's name or marks.
- ✨ **Tenant-connected WhatsApp storefront and sales assistant** — added 2026-10-18: Connect a business-owned WhatsApp number through Meta Embedded Signup, answer from current Commerce records, take simple orders and eligible payments in chat, fall back to hosted checkout for product options or proof upload, and hand conversations to staff. Optional AI support is disabled by default; Meta app review and permissions are required before production onboarding.
- ✨ **Shared realtime Commerce, Inventory and Finance alert tray** — added 2026-09-24: Commerce, Delivery, Inventory and Finance attention share one permission-aware tray. Device push sends committed operational changes, retries durable deliveries and checks access again before transport; Finance summaries cover unpaid invoices, receivables, payables and balancing issues.
- ✨ **Founder-controlled subscription plans & trial policy** — added 2026-09-21: Control plan pricing, module entitlements, the general free-trial duration and per-business founder trial extensions without deleting tenant history or overlapping active paid/lifetime access.
- ✨ **Founder-controlled third-party integration availability** — added 2026-09-20: Optional provider connectors can be hidden and execution-blocked platform-wide without deleting tenant credentials or history; businesses still activate their own approved provider accounts only after configuration.
- ✨ **Plan-upgrade data continuity** — added 2026-09-20: Hidden interconnected modules do not stop required back-office synchronization, so upgrades reveal already-complete records.
- ✨ **Reports, exports and tenant backup** — added 2026-09-25: Export operational history across tax, Books, payroll, Commerce, WhatsApp and stock; tenant backup v2 remaps restored references and disables outbound automation and plaintext credentials.
- ✨ **Surface-safe Online, POS and Distribution pricing** — added 2026-09-20: Physical Store/direct pricing stays inside the staff POS, while hosted/headless commerce exposes Online plus Distribution/Bulk and INPROFIC enforces the chosen channel and minimum quantity server-side.
- ✨ **Standard portions, composed products and Bulk Packs** — added 2026-09-20: Keep production in base units such as scoops/pieces/ml while customers buy plates, servings, bowls, cartons or other packs; show customer-safe package contents and preserve private yield conversions.
- ✨ **Founder product and lead analytics** — added 2026-09-24: Track first-party signup leads, completed registrations, logins, subscription milestones and throttled module usage in Founder Console, with retained deleted-state and permanent-removal controls for signup mailing-list entries, without recording passwords, payment secrets or arbitrary clickstreams.
- ✨ **Animated, consistent form validation across INPROFIC** — added 2026-09-20: HTML5 and Django validation share animated field states, contextual SVG affordances, accessible feedback and consistent toggle switches; dynamic rows inherit the same behavior.
- ✨ **Channel-aware plain and add-on product options** — added 2026-09-20: Offer Extra Jollof, Single Chicken, bottles, pieces or other plain portions from the same Finished Good stock and recipe with independent Physical Store, Online and Bulk/Distribution availability and pricing.
- ✨ **Interactive product demos** — added 2026-09-21: Explore five service profiles, nine logo-connected modules with keyboard-accessible previews, and autoplaying general/storefront walkthroughs. The stock-to-sale example follows the selected service. Illustrative data, reduced-motion support and shared light/dark themes keep the demonstrations clear.
- ✨ **Targeted business mailing workspace** — added 2026-09-24: Create reusable branded email topics, segment current businesses by service and plan, send immediately, and retry provider failures safely with per-recipient delivery totals.
- ✨ **Founder-managed trusted-business strip** — added 2026-09-25: Show a founder-enabled scrolling logo strip above the product demo, automatically limiting tenant logos to paid businesses while the animated platform count includes trials and approved manual logos fill gaps.
- ✨ **Online stock-or-made fulfilment choice** — added 2026-09-27: Customers can choose available Physical Store stock or made-to-order fulfilment per Online line while Online pricing stays unchanged; readiness is shown per product and checkout uses the slowest line.
- ✨ **Routed multi-order rider batches** — added 2026-09-27: Assign multiple customer orders to one rider, organise stop order before pickup, start pickup-and-en-route countdowns together, and keep each customer on a stop-adjusted ETA with active delivery messaging visible to dispatch.
- ✨ **Dispatcher-assigned independent riders** — added 2026-10-09: Keep the in-house delivery flow without riders or a courier integration: the dispatch manager enters the rider's name, phone and vehicle on the order and moves it from assigned to picked up to delivered as the ride progresses. Customers see the rider and each status update exactly as they would for an in-house rider. A dispatcher can also replace an in-house rider with an independent one, and the previous rider is told the job is no longer theirs. The same works for multi-order batches: dispatch can give a whole batch to an independent rider, save its route and mark it picked up, so every customer's ETA starts as it would for an in-house rider.
- ✨ **Choose car or motorbike delivery** — added 2026-10-09: Businesses can offer car delivery beside motorbike delivery just by adding a car price band, with the same riders, independent-rider and batch options. At checkout the customer presses Calculate delivery, sees Motorbike and Car with their own prices and ETAs, picks the one they want, then presses Place order; tracking, rider and dispatch screens show a car or motorbike icon throughout the delivery.
- ✨ **One production order, several channels** — added 2026-10-16: Assign one production order's output, product by product, to Physical Store stock, online or bulk/distribution customers, or market stock - each customer with its own sale and payment - while materials are released once and every batch keeps its cost snapshot. It works whatever the production basis, and whoever entered the order.
- ✨ **Ledger cut-over and Executive Dashboard** — added 2026-11-04: Set a live ledger start date, preserve permission-controlled backlog records, and explore existing datasets in the Executive dashboard with section navigation and normal page scrolling alongside the Basic view.
- ✨ **Holistic Daily Brief and Weekly Summary with executive snapshots** — added 2026-11-04: Choose daily and weekly report sections independently. Review stock risks, sales, canonical P&L, cash and named supplier/vendor balances plus vertical-aware operations. Share selected text with a bounded paper-style executive image and authenticated dashboard link, respecting sender permissions.
- ✨ **Double-entry books from operational records** — added 2026-10-28: Open Books from Finance to build a balanced journal, trial balance and statements on demand; keep manual journals across rebuilds and compare book balances with operational records.
- ✨ **VAT reporting and structured e-invoices** — added 2026-10-28: Open Tax & VAT and E-invoices from Finance, configure VAT-inclusive reporting and product treatments, record input VAT, and prepare invoices from recorded Sales for provider submission or manual clearance tracking.
- ✨ **Theme-aware workspace dialogs and searchable selections** — added 2026-10-28: Edit routine records in contained dialogs, keep Django validation and formset behavior, search selection options, confirm destructive actions, and keep logos and visual buttons readable across themes and tenant palettes.
- ✨ **Activity-tray assistant with record-backed custom questions** — added 2026-11-04: Ask in the responsive activity tray; map business-specific questions to existing permission-checked reports and answer them without an AI provider.
- ✨ **Concise daily distribution snapshots with financial cards** — added 2026-11-04: Release several Market Stock products to one customer on one invoice, with atomic stock checks and one combined payment or receivable. Shared product/customer snapshots and detailed text reflect every released product, region/group, remaining stock and the combined invoice balance. Financial cards show expected value after returns, collections and balances when Finance access permits; recorded returns remain separate.
